# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript with no dependencies and no build step. A one-person profile site needs no framework. Zero dependencies keeps it fast, secure and free to host on GitHub Pages, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages straight from the repository.

## Users

Primary: people near the technician whose computer has a problem (slow, won't boot, virus, broken screen, needs an upgrade or a new build) and who are deciding whether to trust this person with it. They usually arrive from a link the technician shares (WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook bio, Google Maps profile, a business card), most often on a phone.

## Product Purpose

A personal profile and portfolio site for an independent PC repair technician. It shows who they are, what they fix, how the process works and how to get in touch. Success: a visitor understands within seconds what this person repairs and contacts them.

## Positioning

A person, not a shop chain: one technician who explains what is wrong in plain language, shows their work and answers directly. The exact differentiators (turnaround, warranty, home visits, pricing policy) are undecided and must not be invented.

## Operating Context

Visitors check the site on a phone while their computer is out of action, often from a messaging app. Contact happens outside the site, mainly by WhatsApp, phone or email.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: Spanish only (Rioplatense register: "vos").
- Undecided, to be supplied by the owner: real name, city or service area, phone/WhatsApp, email, social links, years of experience, prices, warranty terms, opening hours, real job photos.
- Until supplied, the site uses clearly marked placeholders that are easy to find and replace in one place.

## Brand Commitments

None. Name, logo, colours and style were explicitly delegated to the designer.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet: no photos, testimonials, reviews, client counts or certifications. Future work must not invent testimonials, ratings, client numbers, years of experience, prices or guarantees. Portfolio examples may be shown only as labelled examples until real jobs replace them.

## Product Principles

1. Contact in one tap: the way to reach the technician is never more than a thumb away on mobile.
2. Plain language over jargon: describe symptoms the visitor recognises ("se apaga solo", "tarda en prender"), not part numbers.
3. Show the work: trust comes from a visible, step-by-step process and real repairs, not adjectives.
4. Honest by default: no fabricated proof; placeholders stay obviously placeholders.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Visitors include non-technical and older users: readable sizes, high contrast, large tap targets, respects reduced motion.
